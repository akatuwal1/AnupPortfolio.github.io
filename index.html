FairBERT — debiasing BERT for mental-health text

A BERT classifier fine-tuned with a fairness objective for mental-health text classification. It reaches 84% accuracy and 68.8% F1-macro while reducing the demographic parity gap by 25.6% relative to a vanilla BERT baseline (0.270 → 0.201) — fairness gained without giving up task performance.
